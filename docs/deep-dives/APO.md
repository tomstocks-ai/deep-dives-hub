## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Apollo Global Management is one of the world's largest alternative asset managers, with $858B in fee-earning assets under management. The company operates across three interconnected businesses: Asset Management (private equity, credit, real assets), Retirement Services (via Athene, its insurance platform), and Capital Solutions (principal finance, infrastructure credit). Apollo's structural advantage is the convergence of private credit origination with Athene's permanent insurance/retirement capital.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Retirement Services (Athene)** | ~70% | Insurance premiums, spread income from retirement/annuity products |
| **Asset Management** | ~25% | Management fees, performance fees, fund distributions |
| **Capital Solutions** | ~5% | Principal investing, infrastructure credit, origination fees |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- Athene provides sticky, permanent capital (~$300B+ AUM) generating predictable management fees
- Leading private credit franchise — Apollo is the largest private credit originator globally
- "Originate, manage, distribute" model enables capital-light, high-return fee economics
- Infrastructure credit and capital solutions position Apollo at the intersection of AI buildout financing

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** STRONG — Athene's permanent capital + private credit scale create durable competitive advantages

### Management Team Track Record

**Key Executives:**

- **Marc Rowan:** CEO (since 2021); co-founder, architect of the Athene strategy
- **Scott Kleinman:** Co-President; leads equity and hybrid investing
- **Martin Kelly:** CFO
