## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Talen Energy is an independent power producer (IPP) operating ~13 GW of generation capacity, primarily in PJM — the largest U.S. regional transmission organization. The crown jewel is the 2.5 GW Susquehanna nuclear plant, the largest single nuclear facility in PJM, supplemented by the recently acquired Cornerstone natural gas fleet (Waterford, Darby, Lawrenceburg). The company emerged from Chapter 11 in 2023 with a clean balance sheet and has rapidly positioned itself as a beneficiary of AI data center power demand through a landmark co-location deal with Amazon/AWS at Susquehanna.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Nuclear Generation** | ~55% | Susquehanna 2.5 GW nuclear plant — baseload generation and capacity |
| **Natural Gas/Other Generation** | ~45% | Cornerstone fleet + legacy gas/coal plants in PJM |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- Susquehanna nuclear plant provides zero-carbon baseload power highly valued by data center customers
- ~98% of generation in PJM, where capacity prices have surged from $29 to $330/MW-day
- Clean post-bankruptcy balance sheet with ~$1.9B liquidity
- Amazon/AWS co-location deal validates the nuclear + data center thesis

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** STRONG — nuclear generation is scarce, hard to replicate, and increasingly valuable in a power-constrained AI buildout
