## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Securitize Corp. is a Miami-based financial technology company founded in 2017 that operates a regulated platform for tokenizing and managing digital asset securities. The company provides end-to-end infrastructure for issuing, servicing, and trading tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) on blockchain networks. Securitize is a SEC-registered broker-dealer, transfer agent, and alternative trading system (ATS) operator — one of the few firms holding all three licenses simultaneously.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Tokenization** | ~54% | Fees for issuing and managing tokenized securities on behalf of asset managers, funds, and corporations |
| **Asset Servicing** | ~46% | Ongoing servicing, custody, and administration of tokenized assets, including shareholder registry management and compliance |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- **Regulatory moat:** One of only a handful of firms with SEC-registered broker-dealer, transfer agent, and ATS licenses — a significant barrier to entry in the U.S. tokenization market
- **First-mover scale:** ~\$5.0B in assets under management (AUM) onchain as of July 2026, making it the largest tokenization platform globally and the only one above \$4B in AUM
- **Institutional relationships:** BlackRock's BUIDL fund (tokenized money market fund) is Securitize's flagship product, with BUIDL becoming the first tokenized fund to surpass \$500M AUM; Ethena Labs' USDe also allocated \$250M to the fund
- **Multi-chain infrastructure:** Support for Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, and Solana, with plans to expand to additional chains

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** MODERATE — Regulatory licenses provide a real barrier, but the tokenization market is nascent and competition from both traditional financial infrastructure providers (DTCC, Broadridge) and crypto-native platforms (Ondo, Centrifuge) is intensifying.

### Management Team Track Record

- **Jose Francisco Flores Junco:** Chief Financial Officer — Previously CFO at multiple fintech ventures; joined Securitize in 2021

## 8. READABILITY PASS

Securitize is a company that helps Wall Street put stocks, bonds, and funds on the blockchain. Think of it as a digital factory that turns traditional investments into "tokens" — digital coins that represent ownership and can be traded 24/7. The company is one of the few in the U.S. with full regulatory approval to do this, which makes it special. Big names like BlackRock already use Securitize to run their tokenized money market fund (called BUIDL), which has grown to over \$500 million.

The stock just went public in July 2026 through a SPAC merger, raising about \$350 million in cash. Right now, the company is losing money — about \$22 million per quarter — because it's investing heavily in growth and dealing with the costs of being a new public company. Revenue is around \$14 million per quarter and actually declined slightly year-over-year in Q2 2026.

The bet here is that tokenization — putting real-world assets on the blockchain — becomes a massive trend over the next 5-10 years, and Securitize, as the early leader with regulatory approval, captures a big piece of that market. The risks are that regulation slows down, competitors catch up, or the crypto market enters another winter. This is a speculative growth stock suitable for investors who believe in the long-term tokenization thesis and can handle significant volatility.
