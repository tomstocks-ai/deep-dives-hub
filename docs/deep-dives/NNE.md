## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

NANO Nuclear Energy is developing two portable microreactor designs — ZEUS (a solid-core battery reactor, ~1–5 MW) and ODIN (a low-pressure coolant reactor, ~5–10 MW) — targeting remote power, military, mining, and disaster relief applications. The company also operates HALEU Energy Fuel Inc., a HALEU fuel transportation subsidiary, and has a growing nuclear consulting services business.
