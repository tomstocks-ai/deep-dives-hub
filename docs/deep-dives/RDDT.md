# RDDT — Reddit, Inc.

Reddit is the internet's largest corpus of authentic human discussion — 26+ billion posts and comments, 130.3 million daily active uniques, 514.6 million weekly active uniques — monetised through an advertising business growing 64% year-over-year and a small but strategically loaded AI data-licensing line. The operating story has rarely been better: Q2 2026 revenue of \$805M grew 61% (the eighth consecutive quarter above 60%), gross margin was 91.3%, net income was \$253M at a 31% margin, and free cash flow was \$261M. The stock story is the opposite: RDDT closed at \$143.08 on 2026-09-28, down 40.9% over one year and 46% below its 52-week high of \$263.50, because management disclosed that Google "search referrals were choppy" and US daily uniques grew only 6% while logged-in US uniques grew 1%. The entire debate compresses into one question — is Reddit a durable destination that happens to get Google traffic, or a Google traffic derivative that happens to sell ads?

## Company Overview

**Business model.** Reddit operates a network of ~100,000+ active interest-based communities (subreddits) moderated by unpaid volunteers. Revenue comes from two lines:

- **Advertising — \$762M in Q2 2026 (95% of revenue), up 64% year-over-year.** Self-serve and managed auction-based ads sold against community context and, increasingly, conversational intent. Growth is driven far more by price and ad load improvements than by users: global ARPU rose 36% to \$6.18, US ARPU rose 51% to \$11.85.
- **Other revenue — \$43M in Q2 2026 (5% of revenue), up 24%.** Predominantly AI data licensing, with Google and OpenAI as the two largest counterparties. Small in dollars, oversized in narrative weight.

**Geographic mix.** US revenue was \$638M (+56%); international was \$167M (+84%). International is the growth engine on users (international DAUq +28% to 77.1M) while the US is the monetisation engine (US ARPU is 5.2x international ARPU of \$2.26). The unmonetised international base is the clearest long-duration revenue reservoir.

**The moat, honestly assessed.** Reddit's asset is a two-decade archive of genuine human opinion that cannot be synthesised and is hard to replicate — which is precisely why it ranks so well in search and why LLM builders pay for it. The vulnerability is distribution: of 130.3M daily uniques, 77.7M are logged-out (+27% year-over-year), and logged-out users arrive largely via search. Logged-in DAUq is only 52.6M globally (+7%) and 23.1M in the US (+1%). The durable, owned audience is therefore roughly 40% of the reported user base, and the growing 60% is rented from Google.

**Management and capital.** Co-founder Steve Huffman remains CEO; COO Jen Wong runs monetisation; CFO Drew Vollero. Capital allocation turned shareholder-friendly in 2026: a \$1B repurchase authorisation announced February 2026, with 1.5 million shares bought in Q2 2026 for \$235M at an average of \$157.57 — above the current price. Fully diluted share count was 207.0 million as of 2026-06-30, up only 0.2% year-over-year, so buybacks are more than offsetting stock-based compensation dilution.

**Fiscal 2025 (the last completed year):** revenue of ~\$2.2B grew 69%; net income was \$530M (24% of revenue) — Reddit's first full profitable year; operating cash flow was \$691M and free cash flow \$684M, more than triple the prior year. Q4 2025 alone did \$726M of revenue (+70%) at 91.9% gross margin with \$252M of net income.

**Operating leverage is the standout.** Q2 2026 total costs and expenses rose only 33% (\$573M from \$432M) against 61% revenue growth. Research and development grew 18% and general and administrative grew 10%; only sales and marketing was pushed hard, up 62% to \$196M. Revenue per employee crossed \$1M. This is the profile of a platform that has finished building its ad stack and is now harvesting it.

**Balance sheet (2026-06-30).** Cash and equivalents \$1.487B plus marketable securities \$1.300B equals \$2.786B of liquidity against \$351M of total liabilities and **no debt**. Total assets were \$3.637B; stockholders' equity \$3.286B. Accumulated deficit narrowed to \$214M from \$671M at year-end 2025, and will be erased within roughly two more quarters at the current earnings run-rate. Interest income alone contributed \$25M in the quarter.

**Cash conversion is genuine.** Free cash flow was 32.4% of Q2 revenue and 38.9% of first-half revenue. Capital expenditure is trivial (\$1.1M in the quarter) because Reddit rents infrastructure rather than owning data centres — a rare asset-light profile in 2026. Stock-based compensation of \$101M in the quarter is the one large non-cash gap between GAAP and adjusted figures, and it is roughly 12.5% of revenue, trending down.

**What the financials do not show.** Nothing in the income statement captures the traffic risk. Reddit's costs are fixed and its revenue is user-driven, so a genuine referral decline would hit revenue at a ~90% incremental margin — the operating leverage that looks so attractive on the way up works symmetrically on the way down.

## Valuation

At \$143.08 (2026-09-28), market capitalisation is ~\$28B (~\$27.5B on ~192M Class A and Class B shares outstanding; ~\$29.6B on the 207.0M fully diluted count). Netting \$2.786B of cash and securities against zero debt gives an enterprise value of ~\$25B.

| Multiple | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Trailing P/E | ~33x | On trailing EPS of ~\$4.30 |
| Forward P/E (FY2026E) | ~27x | Consensus FY2026 EPS ~\$5.27, not company-guided |
| Forward P/E (FY2027E) | ~21x | Consensus FY2027 EPS ~\$6.94, not company-guided |
| EV / TTM revenue | ~9x | TTM revenue ~\$2.8B |
| EV / FY2026E revenue | ~7.4x | Consensus ~\$3.3–3.4B, not company-guided |
| FCF yield (annualised Q2) | ~3.7% | \$261M quarterly FCF on ~\$28B cap |

**Framing.** A business compounding revenue above 60% with 31% net margins, 91% gross margins and no debt at 21x next year's earnings is not an expensive stock by any conventional screen. The market is not disputing the multiple arithmetic; it is disputing the denominator's durability. Consensus is not: 34 analysts polled by S&P Global carry a Buy consensus with a \$213.71 average target, MarketBeat's 31-analyst panel shows \$219.29 (high \$300, low \$110), and Morgan Stanley's Brian Nowak trimmed to \$210 from \$240 in September 2026 while keeping Overweight. The average target sits ~49% above spot — an unusually wide gap that signals unresolved disagreement rather than consensus conviction.

**Scenarios:**

- **Bull — \$230–\$270.** Search referrals stabilise, international ARPU (currently \$2.26) starts closing on the US (\$11.85), AI licensing renews at a materially higher rate than the reported ~\$60M/year Google deal, and FY2027 EPS lands near or above \$7 on a re-rated 33–38x. Equates to ~1.6–1.9x the current price.
- **Base — \$165–\$195.** Growth decelerates from 61% toward 35–40% through 2027 as the search-referral tailwind flattens, FY2027 EPS reaches roughly \$6.50–\$7.00, and the multiple settles at 25–28x — no re-rating, just earnings delivery. TIKR consensus for 2028 revenue of ~\$5.5B is consistent with this path.
- **Bear — \$95–\$120.** Logged-out daily uniques turn negative as AI answer engines absorb query volume, US logged-in growth stays near zero, advertising growth falls below 25%, licensing renewals disappoint or lapse, and a 15–18x multiple on flat-to-modest EPS applies. The 52-week low of \$119.27 sits inside this band, so the bear case is already partially rehearsed.

A useful anchor: the base-case midpoint of ~\$180 is roughly where the 200-day moving average (\$170.20) and the pre-Q2-selloff trading range converge.

## Growth Catalysts

- **International monetisation.** 77.1M international daily uniques (+28%) at \$2.26 ARPU versus 53.2M US uniques at \$11.85. Closing even a third of that gap is worth more than \$1B of annualised revenue and requires no new users.
- **AI licensing repricing.** Reddit publicly signalled it may not renew Google's 2024 agreement — reported at ~\$60M/year — on the old terms, and both the Google and OpenAI arrangements come up for renewal in the 2026–2027 window. At \$43M of quarterly "other" revenue against what buyers now pay for scarce human text, the upside optionality is asymmetric. A materially larger, multi-year renewal is the single cleanest re-rating catalyst.
- **Ad product depth.** Shopping ad tools, conversation-level targeting and improved measurement launched through 2026 are what drove 51% US ARPU growth without user growth. Reddit is still early on performance advertising relative to Meta or AppLovin.
- **Legal leverage over scrapers.** In late July 2026 a federal judge largely denied SerpApi's motion to dismiss, letting Reddit's anti-circumvention claims against Perplexity AI and SerpApi proceed. A favourable outcome would strengthen Reddit's ability to charge for — rather than leak — its corpus.
- **Buyback at depressed prices.** \$1B authorised February 2026, with Q2 purchases executed at \$157.57 average. Buying below \$145 is meaningfully accretive at ~21x forward earnings, and management has both the cash (\$2.8B) and the FCF (~\$1B annualised) to be aggressive.
- **Q3 2026 print on 2026-10-29.** Guidance of \$860–870M revenue and \$385–395M adjusted EBITDA. The market will ignore both and read the DAUq table first.

## Risk Factors

**Business risks**

- **Search-referral dependency — the dominant risk.** 77.7M of 130.3M daily uniques are logged out and arrive substantially via Google. Management's own "search referrals were choppy" language triggered the largest single-day decline in the stock's history. As Google shifts to AI-generated answers, the funnel that has driven Reddit's user growth structurally narrows.
- **AI answer engines as substitutes.** Third-party tracking (PromptWatch data cited by on-page.ai, mid-August 2026) reported Reddit's share of ChatGPT Search citations falling from ~3.8% to below 1% — an estimate from an outside vendor, not a company disclosure, and it should be treated as directional only. The underlying risk is real regardless of the precise figure: if answer engines summarise Reddit without linking to it, Reddit supplies the value and captures none of the traffic.
- **US user stagnation.** US DAUq +6%, US logged-in DAUq +1%, US WAUq +9%. The highest-ARPU geography is close to saturated, so US revenue growth of 56% is almost entirely price. Price-led growth has a ceiling.
- **Advertising cyclicality and concentration.** 95% of revenue is advertising. Reddit is a discretionary line item for brand and performance budgets alike and would be cut early in a downturn.

**Financial risks**

- **Symmetric operating leverage.** With ~90% gross margins and a fixed cost base, revenue misses flow to earnings at near-full rate. A deceleration to 25% growth would compress EPS growth far more than the revenue line suggests.
- **Multiple compression on a growth narrative.** The stock lost 40% in a year without a single quarterly miss. That is the market re-rating the durability of growth, and it can continue independently of reported results.
- **Stock-based compensation.** \$101M per quarter (~12.5% of revenue) flatters adjusted EBITDA relative to GAAP and requires continuous buybacks to hold share count flat.

**Governance and sentiment risks**

- **Dual-class structure.** Class A / Class B / Class C share classes leave founders and early holders with disproportionate voting control; public shareholders have limited ability to force change.
- **Insider selling.** Huffman sold 17,308 shares for ~\$2.8M in a filing disclosed 2026-09-26, after the stock's 39% one-year decline. Likely plan-based, but poorly timed optics.
- **Moderator and community dependence.** Content is produced and policed by unpaid volunteers. Policy changes, API disputes or moderator revolts have disrupted the platform before and are a genuine operational hazard.

## Recommendation

**Rating: SPEC. BUY.** The fundamentals justify a BUY on their own; the unresolved structural question on traffic durability is why this carries the speculative qualifier rather than a clean one.

**The case in one paragraph.** You are paying ~21x FY2027 consensus earnings and ~7.4x FY2026 consensus revenue for a debt-free business with \$2.8B net cash, 91% gross margins, 31% net margins, 60%+ revenue growth, ~\$1B of annualised free cash flow and a buyback active below the price at which management last bought. In exchange you accept that roughly 60% of the reported user base is rented from a landlord (Google) who is actively changing the terms, and that nobody — including management — can yet quantify how much of that traffic survives the shift to AI answers.

**Position sizing.** Treat as a 1.5–2.5% position in a diversified growth sleeve, not a core holding. The distribution of outcomes is genuinely wide: the bull case is ~1.7x and the bear case is ~0.75x from here.

**Entry strategy.** Scale in thirds rather than taking a single position. The stock trades below both its 50-day (\$158.75) and 200-day (\$170.20) moving averages, so the trend is still against you.

- First tranche at \$140–\$148 (current zone).
- Second tranche at \$120–\$128, near the 52-week low of \$119.27 — the level where the bear case is substantially priced.
- Final tranche only on confirmation: a reclaim of the 50-day average on a quarter showing stabilising US and logged-out daily uniques.

**Stop loss.** A close below \$112 invalidates the thesis — it would break the 52-week low and signal that the market is pricing structural decline rather than deceleration. For a thesis-based rather than price-based exit: two consecutive quarters of declining logged-out DAUq.

**Technical context.** Spot \$143.08 sits 20% below the 50-day and 16% below the 200-day moving average, with the 50-day beneath the 200-day — a downtrend, not a base. Support is the 52-week low at \$119.27; first resistance is the 50-day at ~\$159, then the \$170–\$180 zone where the 200-day and the pre-selloff range overlap. Nothing in the chart is bullish yet, which is why staged entry matters more than usual here.

## Sentiment Analysis

**News tone — negative, with the pessimism concentrated on one issue.** Coverage since late July 2026 is remarkably uniform: the quarter was excellent and the stock was destroyed anyway. Headlines ranged from "Reddit stock tanks 21% on 'choppy' Google search referrals" to "Reddit Stock Collapses 23% as AI Eats Away at User Growth" and "RDDT Stock Set For Worst Day On Record." The 247wallst framing — Alphabet up 6% while Reddit fell 23% on the same day — captures how the market has reassigned the value of Reddit's data from Reddit to the AI platforms consuming it.

**Sell-side — constructive but retreating.** Consensus remains Buy / Moderate Buy with average targets of \$213.71 (34 analysts, S&P Global) and \$219.29 (31 analysts, MarketBeat), spanning \$110 to \$300. The direction of revisions matters more than the level: Morgan Stanley cut \$240 to \$210 in September 2026 while keeping Overweight, and the pattern across the street is "lower the target, keep the rating." Analysts still believe; they are just marking down how much they will pay for the belief.

**Retail and social — capitulation, with a wrinkle.** Reddit's own investor subreddit r/RDDT is unusually well informed because the company uses it for Reg FD disclosure, and the tone there through August and September 2026 has shifted from defending the growth story to debating whether the platform's traffic model is structurally broken. The stock's 40.9% one-year decline and the fact that shares sit 46% below the 52-week high have flushed out momentum holders. Notably, the bear argument on retail forums is now the same as the institutional one — a sign the fear is well understood rather than novel, which typically limits further downside from the same catalyst.

**Positioning and flow.** Thirteen-F activity through September 2026 shows trimming rather than exit (for example, Envestnet Asset Management reduced its holding in a 2026-09-29 filing). Options data was not available at the level of granularity needed for a reliable read in this session, so no inference is drawn from flow. The CEO's 17,308-share sale for ~\$2.8M, disclosed 2026-09-26, is small relative to his holdings and most likely plan-based, but it did not help sentiment.

**Sentiment score: 3.5 / 10 — bearish.** Price action, news tone and retail mood are all negative; sell-side ratings and the company's own buyback are the offsetting positives. For a contrarian, that combination — universally understood bear case, decelerating but still 60% growth, insider-level buyback below the current price — is closer to an opportunity than a warning. For a momentum investor, there is nothing here yet.

## Readability Pass

**In plain English:** Reddit makes almost all of its money selling ads inside its communities, and it is doing that extremely well — revenue grew 61% last quarter, it keeps 31 cents of every dollar as profit, it has \$2.8 billion in the bank and no debt.

The problem is where its visitors come from. Roughly six of every ten daily visitors are not logged in — they land on Reddit after searching for something on Google. Google is now answering more questions itself with AI instead of sending people to websites. Reddit told investors those referrals had become "choppy," and its US visitor growth slowed to almost nothing. The stock fell 23% in one day on that sentence and is down 41% over the year, even though every financial number beat expectations.

So the stock is cheap relative to its growth — about 21 times next year's expected earnings for a business growing at 60% — precisely because investors are not sure the growth is Reddit's to keep. If Reddit's own app and its untapped international audience can replace the Google funnel, the shares are worth substantially more (\$230–\$270). If the funnel keeps narrowing, they are worth substantially less (\$95–\$120). The middle path, where growth slows but the business keeps compounding, puts the shares at \$165–\$195.

**How to act on it:** buy a partial position now, keep cash for \$120–\$128, and let the quarterly user numbers — not the revenue headline — tell you whether to complete the position. The one number that decides this thesis is logged-out daily active uniques. Watch it every quarter.
