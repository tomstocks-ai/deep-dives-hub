## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Lam Research is a global supplier of wafer fabrication equipment and services to the semiconductor industry. The company is the dominant player in etch and thin-film deposition — two of the most critical and frequently used process steps in semiconductor manufacturing. Lam's equipment is essential for 3D NAND layer stacking, DRAM/HBM manufacturing, and advanced logic GAA transistors.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Systems** | ~65% | Etch, deposition, and clean equipment for wafer fabs |
| **Customer Support** | ~35% | Spares, upgrades, services — high-margin recurring revenue |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- #1 in plasma etch and #1 or #2 in deposition — the most process-step-intensive WFE categories
- Every additional 3D NAND layer and every GAA transistor requires more etch/dep steps
- Installed base of >80,000 chambers drives high-margin recurring services
- AI workloads structurally increase demand for NAND/HBM/logic, directly expanding Lam's TAM

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** STRONG — dominant market position in the two fastest-growing WFE categories
