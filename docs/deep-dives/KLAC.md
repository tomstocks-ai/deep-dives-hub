## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

KLA Corporation is the world's leading supplier of process control and yield management solutions for the semiconductor industry. Its inspection and metrology tools detect defects and measure critical dimensions at every step of chip manufacturing, making them essential for achieving viable yields at advanced nodes. KLA also serves the PCB, display, and advanced packaging markets.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Semiconductor Process Control** | ~85% | Wafer inspection, reticle inspection, metrology, data analytics |
| **Specialty Semiconductor / PCB / Display** | ~15% | Advanced packaging inspection, PCB, and flat panel display |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- ~55% share of semiconductor process control — closest competitor (Applied Materials) has ~15%
- Process control is the least cyclical WFE segment because defect detection is non-optional
- Rising inspection intensity at advanced nodes (EUV, GAA, 3D NAND) structurally grows KLA's SAM
- Advanced packaging process control (~$1.1B in CY2026) is a new growth vector from HBM/chiplets
- Highest gross margins (~63%) in WFE, supporting superior capital returns

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** STRONG — near-monopoly in process control with rising content per wafer
