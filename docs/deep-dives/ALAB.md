## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Astera Labs is a fabless semiconductor company that designs and sells connectivity solutions for cloud and AI data center infrastructure. The company addresses signal integrity and link management challenges that arise as server architectures incorporate higher-bandwidth processors and accelerators.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **PCIe/CXL Retimers & Smart Cables** | ~70% | Signal integrity ICs and active cable modules for GPU/accelerator connectivity |
| **Fabric Switches & Software** | ~30% | Intelligent fabric switches and management software for AI cluster interconnects |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- First-mover in PCIe 5.0/6.0 retimer silicon with broad hyperscaler qualifications
- Intelligent Connectivity Platform integrates hardware, firmware, and management software
- Deep co-engineering relationships with NVIDIA, AMD, and hyperscaler ASIC teams
- CXL 2.0/3.0 memory pooling solutions position the company for an emerging TAM

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** MODERATE — technology leadership in a fast-growing niche, but larger rivals (Broadcom, Marvell) compete in adjacent connectivity markets

### Management Team Track Record

**Key Executives:**

- **Jitendra Mohan:** CEO & Co-founder (since founding in 2017); previously VP at Texas Instruments
- **Sanjay Gajendra:** VP/Co-founder; ex-Intel/Marvell connectivity architect
