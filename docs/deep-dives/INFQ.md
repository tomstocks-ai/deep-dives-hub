## Company Overview

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Infleqtion, Inc. (formerly ColdQuanta, Inc.) is a global leader in **neutral-atom quantum technology**, spanning quantum computing, quantum sensing, and quantum software. Founded in 2007 and headquartered in Louisville, Colorado, the company went public in early 2026 via a SPAC merger with Churchill Capital Corp X, becoming the **first neutral-atom quantum company to list on a major exchange**.

The company runs a full-stack platform across hardware (quantum computers, sensors, photonic components) and software (the Superstaq quantum platform and contextual machine-learning tools). Unlike most quantum pure-plays that remain purely R&D-stage, Infleqtion has **active commercial revenue** from quantum sensing products and has delivered quantum computers to national labs and government customers.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown (approximate):**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Quantum Sensing** | ~60% | Optical atomic clocks, quantum RF receivers, inertial navigation systems |
| **Quantum Computing** | ~25% | Neutral-atom quantum computers, testbeds, and software licenses |
| **Services and Software** | ~15% | Superstaq platform, consulting, and integration services |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

- **Neutral-atom differentiation:** the only publicly traded pure-play using neutral atoms (vs. trapped ions at IonQ, superconducting at IBM/Google). Neutral atoms offer room-temperature operation potential and lower environmental noise.
- **235+ patents** issued and pending, creating meaningful IP barriers.
- **First-mover in commercial quantum sensing:** revenue-generating clocks, RF sensors, and navigation systems already deployed with defense customers provide cash flow while computing scales.
- **NVIDIA partnership:** collaboration on hybrid quantum-classical workloads, with NVIDIA acting as both partner and customer.
- **Government anchor:** three DOE "Genesis Mission" projects and DARPA/DoD contracts reinforce a durable government/defense revenue base — recently underscored by CEO Matthew Kinsella testifying before the House Committee on Natural Resources.

**Moat Assessment:** MODERATE — strong IP and technology differentiation, but the quantum computing market is nascent. The sensing revenue stream is the near-term moat that pure computing competitors lack.

### Management Team

- **Matthew Kinsella — CEO.** Venture background from Maverick Capital; led the founding investment and drove the public-company transition. Recently testified before Congress on quantum's strategic importance.
- **Dana Anderson — Chief Science Officer and Founder.** Physics/EE professor at CU Boulder and JILA fellow; developed the first ultracold-atom chip.
- **Pranav Gokhale — CTO and Co-founder.** Owns the technical roadmap toward the 100 logical-qubit 2028 target.
- **Finance and commercial leadership** manages the public-company transition and government/commercial sales expansion.
