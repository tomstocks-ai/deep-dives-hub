# TECK — Teck Resources Limited

Teck Resources is a Canadian diversified miner that, following the 2024 sale of its steelmaking-coal business to Glencore, is now a pure-play "green metals" producer of copper and zinc. A record Q2 2026 — adjusted EBITDA roughly tripled year-over-year to about \$2.2B on record copper prices and higher production — and a pending "merger of equals" with Anglo American underpin a strong longer-term bull case. But after a ~23% run to ~\$59.75 the stock now trades above the blended analyst consensus 12-month target of ~\$53.23, and the consensus label is Hold. We move our rating from BUY to **HOLD**: the copper supercycle and Anglo Teck story remain intact, but near-term risk/reward is balanced.

**Report Date:** July 28, 2026 · **Rating:** HOLD · **Price:** \$59.75 (2026-07-28)

---

## Company Overview

Teck Resources Limited (NYSE: TECK; TSX: TECK.A / TECK.B) is a Vancouver-based diversified mining company with roots stretching back over a century in Canadian resource development. After exiting oil sands (2022) and divesting its steelmaking-coal business to Glencore for \$7.3B in 2024, Teck reoriented itself as a focused copper-and-zinc producer positioned squarely for the electrification of the global economy.

**Revenue segments and assets:**

- **Copper** — the growth engine and majority of EBITDA. Key assets include Quebrada Blanca (QB2) in Chile, Highland Valley Copper in British Columbia, a 22.5% stake in Antamina (Peru), and Carmen de Andacollo (Chile).
- **Zinc** — a stable cash generator anchored by the world-class Red Dog mine in Alaska and the integrated Trail smelting and refining operations in British Columbia, where a strategic-metals initiative is advancing.

**Moat.** Teck's competitive edge rests on long-life, low-cost Tier 1 orebodies in stable-to-moderate-risk jurisdictions (Canada, plus Chile and Peru), decades of operating expertise, and a fortress balance sheet built from the coal-sale proceeds. Copper is a structurally scarce, hard-to-permit commodity, and Teck's producing base plus its growth pipeline give it durable exposure to a metal central to EVs, renewables, grid buildout, and AI data-center power demand.

**Management.** The leadership team has executed a disciplined transformation — divesting coal, ramping QB2, and negotiating the Anglo American merger of equals — while reaffirming production guidance and returning capital to shareholders.

## Growth Catalysts

- **Anglo American "merger of equals" (Anglo Teck).** The central catalyst. The combination would create a top-five global copper producer with ~\$800M in annual synergies and expanded Tier 1 copper scale in the Americas. Progress toward close continues, though regulatory approvals across jurisdictions and integration execution remain gating items, and timing is the key swing factor.
- **Copper supercycle demand.** Electrification (EVs, renewables), grid modernization, and AI data-center power demand underpin a structural, multi-year copper deficit that supports pricing and Teck's long-dated growth optionality.
- **QB2 (Quebrada Blanca) ramp.** Teck's flagship Chilean copper asset continues ramping toward higher output, a direct driver of volume and unit-cost improvement.
- **Highland Valley Copper mine-life extension.** Extends the life of Teck's cornerstone Canadian copper operation, adding durable production.
- **Trail strategic-metals initiative.** Leverages the integrated Trail smelting/refining complex toward higher-value strategic and critical metals.
- **Reaffirmed 2026-2028 production guidance.** Provides a credible, near-term production growth path that management has stood behind through Q2 2026.

## Risk Factors

- **Valuation risk.** After a ~23% run, the stock trades above the blended consensus 12-month target (~\$53.23), leaving less margin of safety and raising the odds of multiple compression if sentiment cools.
- **Copper-price and China-demand cyclicality.** Teck's earnings are highly geared to copper. A China demand slowdown or global recession could correct prices sharply and compress cash flow.
- **Merger execution and regulatory risk.** The Anglo Teck combination must clear multiple regulatory reviews and integrate two large organizations; delays, conditions, or failure would remove a core pillar of the thesis.
- **Chile political and royalty risk.** A meaningful share of copper production and growth sits in Chile, exposing Teck to royalty increases, permitting, and political/regulatory shifts.
- **Operational / ramp risk.** QB2 and Highland Valley must deliver against reaffirmed guidance; ramp shortfalls or cost inflation would pressure results.
- **FX and reporting complexity.** Teck reports in CAD while trading on the NYSE in USD; currency moves add noise to headline comparisons.

## Recommendation

**Rating: HOLD.** The transformation into a pure-play copper/zinc "green metals" producer, the record Q2 2026 (adjusted EBITDA roughly tripled to ~\$2.2B), the fortress balance sheet, and the Anglo Teck merger all make Teck a high-quality, structurally advantaged copper name. But price has caught up to fundamentals: at ~\$59.75 the stock trades above the blended consensus target of ~\$53.23, the consensus label is Hold, and the shares sit inside our base-case \$55–60 band. That combination argues for patience rather than fresh buying.

**Positioning and entry.** Existing holders can continue to hold and let the merger and copper supercycle thesis play out. New capital is better deployed on pullbacks toward the base-case floor or below, where risk/reward improves toward the bull scenario. Given a beta of 1.59, position sizing should reflect the stock's cyclicality.

## Readability Pass

In plain English: Teck digs copper and zinc out of the ground. Copper is the "electricity metal" — it goes into EVs, power grids, solar and wind, and the data centers behind AI — and the world is heading toward a shortage of it. A few years ago Teck sold its coal business, cleaned up its balance sheet, and became a focused copper company. Its latest quarter was a blowout: profits (EBITDA) roughly tripled because copper prices hit records and Teck produced more. On top of that, Teck is combining with a much larger miner, Anglo American, to become one of the biggest copper producers in the world.

So why not "buy"? Because the stock has already jumped about 23% and now trades a bit higher than what the average Wall Street analyst thinks it's worth over the next year. The long-term story is great, but at today's price you're paying up for it. That's why we say **hold**: keep what you own and let the story play out, and look to add on dips rather than chasing it here.
