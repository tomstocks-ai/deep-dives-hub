# VELO — Velo3D, Inc.

Velo3D is a recapitalized metal additive manufacturing company whose support-free laser powder bed fusion technology enables true design freedom for mission-critical parts across aerospace, space, defense, semiconductor, and energy end markets. Trading at \$10.25 (2026-07-28) for a market cap of ~\$305M, the company is mid-turnaround: new leadership, a pivot to recurring revenue through Rapid Production Solutions (RPS), a newly opened ~289,000 sq ft Livermore "Forge 1" production campus, and FY2026 guidance of \$60–70M revenue with EBITDA positivity targeted for H2 2026. The setup is binary — a credible margin/EBITDA inflection versus a fresh short-report attack and dilution overhang — so this remains a high-risk, high-reward **SPEC. BUY**.

## Company Overview

Velo3D designs and manufactures metal additive manufacturing (AM) systems built around its differentiated **support-free laser powder bed fusion** process. The core platform pairs the **Sapphire** family of printers with the company's **Flow** print-preparation software and **Assure** quality-control system, allowing customers to print complex geometries — low angles, overhangs, internal channels — without the support structures that constrain legacy AM. That "design freedom" is the technical moat: it lets engineers print parts that competing systems cannot, which matters most in mission-critical aerospace, space, defense, semiconductor, and energy components.

The business model is shifting. Historically reliant on lumpy, capital-equipment system sales, Velo3D has been recapitalized under new leadership and is pivoting toward a **recurring-revenue model via Rapid Production Solutions (RPS)** — effectively operating printers to sell qualified parts and production capacity rather than only selling machines. The centerpiece of that pivot is the newly opened **Livermore Production Campus ("Forge 1")**, a roughly 289,000 sq ft facility that is one of North America's largest metal additive manufacturing sites, built to house 100+ printers and to triple production capacity in support of defense and aerospace demand.

Management's stated priorities are clear: ramp Forge 1, convert design freedom into repeat production programs, and reach EBITDA positivity in 2026. The company has also added strategic relationships — including an **Aurelia Technologies partnership (2026-06-09)** for additive manufacturing in next-generation gas turbines — and continues to emphasize aerospace/defense and space customers. The relationship with SpaceX has been a prominent part of the narrative and is now contested (see Risk Factors).

## Financial Analysis

Velo3D is a small, fast-growing, and still deeply unprofitable company. FY2025 revenue was **\$45.97M, up 12.1% YoY**, while trailing-twelve-month revenue has since accelerated to **~\$50.5M (+24.5%)** as the turnaround takes hold. The most encouraging data point is the latest quarter: **Q1 2026 revenue of \$13.8M, up 48% YoY** from \$9.3M, with non-GAAP EPS improving sharply to **-\$0.20** versus -\$0.67 a year earlier.

Profitability remains the central question. FY2025 net loss was **-\$71.36M**, and on a trailing basis net income is **-\$53.35M** with **TTM diluted EPS of -\$2.75**. The company is spending to build its future: FY2026 guidance calls for **revenue of \$60–70M**, **>30% gross margin in H2 2026**, **non-GAAP adjusted opex of \$45–55M**, and **capex of \$40–50M** — the bulk of that capex directed at the RPS build-out and Forge 1. That is a heavy investment program relative to the company's size, which is why the H2 2026 EBITDA-positivity milestone is pivotal: hit it, and the model self-sustains; miss it, and additional financing becomes likely.

Balance-sheet flexibility is thin and dilution is a live theme. The company has **filed to sell 3M shares for existing holders (a resale registration)**, adding an overhang, and the combination of aggressive capex, a still-negative bottom line, and speculative trading dynamics (see below) means investors should assume execution must track guidance closely to avoid a dilutive raise.

## Valuation

At \$10.25 the market cap is ~\$305M on ~29.8M shares outstanding, or roughly **6x FY2025 revenue** (~4x the guided FY2026 midpoint of ~\$65M). For a sub-scale, loss-making hardware company that multiple is only justifiable if the RPS pivot and H2 2026 margin inflection prove the model can scale toward profitability. There is no meaningful earnings or free-cash-flow multiple to anchor to yet, so valuation is a scenario exercise driven by whether guidance is met and whether the short-report controversy resolves.

**Bull case — \$22–28.** FY2026 revenue lands at or above the high end of the \$60–70M guide, gross margin clears 30% in H2, EBITDA turns positive on schedule, Forge 1 ramps toward capacity, and the Morpheus allegations are credibly rebutted. Russell 3000/Microcap inclusion and new sell-side coverage (Needham Buy \$33, Cantor Overweight \$17, Lake Street \$20; Strong Buy consensus, ~\$23.75 average target) broaden the buyer base and support a re-rating.

**Base case — \$13–15.** Revenue lands within the guided range, margins approach the >30% H2 target, EBITDA breakeven arrives roughly on schedule, capex/opex track guidance, and the short-report noise fades without lasting damage. Modest dilution risk lingers until profitability is confirmed.

**Bear case — \$4–6.** The Morpheus short thesis gains traction and damages the SpaceX/defense narrative, EBITDA positivity slips past H2 2026, and the \$40–50M capex plan forces a dilutive raise. The 3M-share resale registration and speculative ETF flows amplify a drawdown, compounded by micro-cap volatility (beta 2.52) and the \$2.81–\$31.75 52-week range.

## Growth Catalysts

- **Forge 1 / Livermore capacity ramp.** The ~289,000 sq ft campus (built for 100+ printers) triples production capacity and is the physical enabler of the RPS recurring-revenue model. Utilization ramping through H2 2026 is the single largest operational catalyst.
- **RPS recurring-revenue traction.** Shifting from lumpy machine sales to selling qualified production capacity could smooth revenue and lift margins — the strategic re-rating thesis.
- **FY2026 revenue guide (\$60–70M).** Delivering ~30%+ growth against a credibility-challenged backdrop would be a strong validation signal.
- **H2 2026 margin and EBITDA inflection.** Clearing >30% gross margin and reaching EBITDA positivity removes the dilution overhang.
- **Defense, space, and aerospace demand.** Support-free geometry wins in mission-critical parts; the Aurelia Technologies partnership (2026-06-09) extends the platform into next-gen gas turbines.
- **Institutional visibility.** Addition to the **Russell 3000 and Russell Microcap indices (effective 2026-06-29)** plus fresh coverage (Needham \$33, Cantor \$17, Lake Street \$20) broadens the potential shareholder base.

## Risk Factors

- **Morpheus Research short report (late June 2026).** The report alleges a **"sham relationship with SpaceX"** and that shares are "rallying on SpaceX hype." This is a material bear risk and a credibility question; if the market gives it weight, the narrative and multiple can compress quickly. Resolution of these allegations is itself a catalyst — in either direction.
- **Financing / dilution.** With FY2026 capex of \$40–50M and a still-negative bottom line (FY2025 net loss -\$71.36M), missing the H2 2026 EBITDA-positivity milestone likely forces a dilutive raise. The **3M-share resale registration** adds a near-term overhang.
- **Speculative trading dynamics.** Defiance launched **VELL, a 2X-long single-stock ETF on VELO (2026-06-05)**, signaling speculative retail interest and structurally amplifying volatility. Beta is **2.52** and the 52-week range spans \$2.81–\$31.75.
- **Execution and lumpiness.** System sales remain lumpy and customer concentration is high; a single slipped program can produce a guidance miss.
- **Turnaround risk.** The recapitalization and RPS pivot are recent and unproven at scale; the recurring-revenue model must actually convert design freedom into repeat production revenue.

## Recommendation

**Rating: SPEC. BUY. Position size 1–3% max, aggressive portfolios only.** Velo3D offers a genuine technology edge and a credible turnaround plan, but the outcome is binary: the H2 2026 margin/EBITDA inflection either lands and de-risks the story, or it slips and forces dilution into a short-report controversy. Scenario targets are **Bull \$22–28**, **Base \$13–15**, and **Bear \$4–6**.

**Entry strategy.** Given beta 2.52 and the VELL 2X ETF amplifying swings, scale in rather than chasing. The stock sits well off its \$31.75 high but far above the \$2.81 low; prior technical work flagged support around \$9.50 and \$8.30 with resistance near \$13.00 and \$16.20 — use weakness toward support for adds and keep the position small. Watch the RSI and moving averages on the chart above for momentum confirmation rather than a separate technical section.

**Stop / risk management.** Because the bear case (\$4–6) implies a large drawdown, size the position so a move to bear-case levels is survivable. A break below the \$8.30 support zone on heavy volume, or credible corroboration of the Morpheus allegations, would be a reason to cut.

**Catalyst calendar.**

- **Q2 2026 earnings — August 26, 2026:** progress against the \$60–70M guide and margin trajectory.
- **H2 2026:** >30% gross margin and EBITDA positivity milestone.
- **H2 2026:** Forge 1 capacity ramp toward 100+ printers.
- **FY2026:** revenue guide execution and RPS recurring-revenue traction.
- **Ongoing:** resolution of the Morpheus short-report allegations.

## Sentiment Analysis

Sentiment is polarized and unusually speculative for a name this size. On the bullish side, Velo3D carries a **Strong Buy analyst consensus with an average 12-month target of ~\$23.75** (Needham Buy \$33, Cantor Overweight \$17, Lake Street raised to \$20), and inclusion in the Russell 3000/Microcap indices has improved institutional visibility. Retail enthusiasm is elevated — evidenced by Defiance launching the **VELL 2X-long single-stock ETF**, a structure that only exists where speculative demand is high.

Countering that, **Morpheus Research's short report** questioning the SpaceX relationship has injected a sharp bearish narrative and a credibility overhang, and the 3M-share resale registration feeds dilution concerns. Options and single-stock-ETF flows point to high implied and realized volatility (beta 2.52), consistent with a battleground stock where every earnings print and press release moves the tape hard.

**Net sentiment score: cautiously positive but contested (roughly 6/10).** The sell-side and index-inclusion tailwinds are real, but the short report and dilution overhang keep conviction speculative. This is a headline-driven stock; treat sentiment as a source of volatility, not a thesis.

## Readability Pass

In plain English: Velo3D makes advanced metal 3D printers that can produce shapes competitors can't, which is valuable for rockets, jets, and defense parts. After a rough stretch, new management raised money, opened a big new factory near San Francisco ("Forge 1"), and is changing the business so it earns steadier, repeat revenue by making parts for customers rather than just selling machines. Sales are growing fast again (up 48% last quarter), and management says the company should stop burning cash at the operating level in the second half of 2026. If that happens, the stock could re-rate meaningfully (bull \$22–28). But it's a gamble: the company is still losing money and spending heavily, it may need to sell more shares, and a short-seller recently accused it of overhyping its SpaceX ties. Keep any position small — this is a speculative bet on a turnaround working out, not a safe holding.
