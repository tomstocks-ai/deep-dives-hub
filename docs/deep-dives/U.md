# U — Unity Software Inc.

Unity Software is the leading real-time 3D content creation platform, powering an estimated 70% of mobile games alongside a growing advertising and monetization business. After a bruising 2023–2025 stretch marked by the "Runtime Fee" controversy and a leadership change, the company is now executing a credible turnaround under CEO Matt Bromberg. The new **Vector** AI-powered advertising platform is reaccelerating the "Grow Solutions" segment, a strong (pre-announced) Q1 2026 helped shares recover roughly 75% off the February/March 2026 lows to ~\$30, and TTM revenue has reaccelerated to +7.6%. With ~\$2B in cash, positive free cash flow, and a Buy consensus, the risk/reward has improved enough to warrant a **SPEC. BUY** — tempered by persistent GAAP losses, heavy stock-based compensation, and a high beta of 2.05.

## Company Overview

### Business Model

Unity operates a platform for creating and operating interactive, real-time 2D and 3D content. Its customers span game developers, artists, and increasingly industrial and advertising clients. The business is organized into two strategic franchises:

| Segment | Description | Role |
|---------|-------------|------|
| **Create Solutions** | Tools to build, ship, and run real-time content — the Unity Engine and authoring stack | Mobile game engine leadership; anchor of the Unity 7 roadmap |
| **Grow Solutions** | User acquisition, engagement, and monetization tools (ads, analytics) | The engine of the current rebuild via the new **Vector** AI platform |

### The Turnaround Under Matt Bromberg

Matt Bromberg, appointed CEO in mid-2024, has centered the strategy on rebuilding developer trust and re-accelerating monetization. The single most important initiative is **Vector**, a new AI-powered advertising and monetization platform that improves ad targeting and yield for developers. Vector is the key driver of the Grow Solutions rebuild, and early results — a strong pre-announced Q1 2026 plus favorable mobile-game industry checks — suggest the turnaround is materializing rather than merely being promised.

On the Create side, Unity unveiled the **Unity 7** roadmap at Unite Seoul: a next-generation authoring platform for creating, deploying, and growing games. Unity 7 is designed to defend and extend Unity's mobile game engine leadership, the franchise's core moat.

### Competitive Moat

- **Market leadership:** ~70% of mobile games are built on Unity.
- **Ecosystem lock-in:** a large asset store, deep documentation, and an entrenched developer community.
- **Cross-platform reach:** particularly strong on mobile, where Unreal is comparatively weaker.
- **Two-sided data advantage:** the Grow/ads business feeds monetization data back into the platform.

### Management

CEO Matt Bromberg leads a team focused on execution discipline after the Runtime Fee episode and the departure of former CEO John Riccitiello. Institutional ownership remains high; insider ownership is relatively low.

## Financial Analysis

### Income Statement

- **FY2025 revenue:** \$1.85B, up +2.0% YoY.
- **FY2025 net loss:** -\$402.77M.
- **TTM revenue:** ~\$1.92B, reaccelerating to +7.6% — the clearest quantitative sign that the Grow rebuild is taking hold.
- **TTM net income:** -\$672.73M (GAAP), elevated by one-time charges and stock-based compensation.
- **TTM diluted EPS:** -\$1.58.
- **Gross margin:** ~74%, consistent with a healthy platform/SaaS profile.

The story in the numbers is a reacceleration of the top line (from +2% FY to +7.6% TTM) even as GAAP losses remain large. Much of the loss is non-cash (SBC) and charge-driven, which is why the company can be free-cash-flow positive while still unprofitable on a GAAP basis.

### Balance Sheet

- **Cash:** ~\$2.0B, providing multi-year runway.
- **Solid liquidity** relative to obligations, though the balance sheet still carries substantial goodwill from prior acquisitions, creating impairment risk.

### Cash Flow

- **Free cash flow:** positive, driven by an asset-light model with minimal capex.
- **Stock-based compensation:** heavy, diluting shareholders roughly 5% annually — a persistent overhang.

*Plain English:* Unity keeps about 74 cents of gross profit per revenue dollar and now generates real cash, but pays employees heavily in stock and still reports large GAAP losses. The improving revenue trajectory is what makes the current setup more attractive than a year ago.

## Risk Factors

### Business Risks

- **Execution risk on the turnaround.** The Vector/Grow rebuild is still early. If the ad ramp disappoints or the mobile ad market weakens, revenue reacceleration could stall.
- **Unreal Engine competition.** Epic's Unreal Engine 5 offers photorealistic graphics and is free below \$1M in revenue, pressuring Unity in high-end and AAA gaming.
- **Developer trust.** Damage from the 2023 Runtime Fee controversy may not fully heal; any pricing missteps would be punished quickly.

### Financial Risks

- **Persistent GAAP losses.** TTM net income of -\$672.73M, elevated by charges and SBC, keeps the company GAAP-unprofitable even with positive FCF.
- **Stock-based compensation.** ~5% annual dilution is a structural headwind to per-share value.
- **Goodwill / impairment risk.** A large goodwill balance from past acquisitions could force write-downs.

### Macro / Sector Risks

- **High beta (2.05).** The stock moves roughly twice the market, amplifying drawdowns in risk-off periods.
- **Gaming and ad-market cyclicality.** Grow Solutions revenue is sensitive to mobile advertising budgets.
- **China exposure** in the gaming market carries regulatory and geopolitical risk.
