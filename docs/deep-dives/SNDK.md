## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

SanDisk Corporation was spun off from Western Digital in February 2026 as a pure-play NAND flash memory and storage company. It designs, manufactures, and sells flash-based storage solutions including enterprise SSDs, client SSDs, removable flash, and embedded flash products.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Enterprise & Cloud** | ~55% | Enterprise SSDs for data centers and AI workloads |
| **Client** | ~25% | Client SSDs for PCs and notebooks |
| **Consumer** | ~20% | Removable flash, embedded flash, USB drives |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- One of three integrated NAND manufacturers with proprietary 3D NAND technology (BiCS)
- Joint venture with Kioxia at Yokkaichi/Kitakami fabs provides manufacturing scale and cost sharing
- Iconic consumer brand with strong retail channel presence
- Enterprise SSD portfolio qualified at major hyperscalers for AI storage workloads

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** MODERATE — integrated manufacturer with scale, but NAND is commoditized and Samsung leads
