# ZS — Zscaler, Inc.

Zscaler is the pure-play leader in cloud-delivered zero trust security, routing enterprise internet and private-application traffic through its globally distributed Zero Trust Exchange to replace legacy firewalls, VPNs, and network appliances. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) is growing in the mid-20% range with record non-GAAP operating margins near 23% and roughly 30% free cash flow margins, and the platform is expanding into securing agentic AI, machine identities, and data. After a sharp selloff from its 52-week high on cautious FY2027 guidance commentary and leadership-transition concerns, the stock trades near \$154 — well below the analyst consensus target — which we view as an attractive entry for a durable secular-growth compounder. Rating: **BUY**.

## Company Overview

Zscaler, Inc. (NASDAQ: ZS) is a cloud security company that pioneered the security service edge (SSE) and zero trust network access (ZTNA) categories. Rather than backhauling traffic to on-premise data centers protected by hardware appliances, Zscaler inspects traffic in its cloud — the Zero Trust Exchange — which processes hundreds of billions of transactions per day across a global network of data centers. Users, devices, workloads, and increasingly AI agents connect to applications through this exchange, which enforces policy, blocks threats, and prevents data loss inline.

The core platform is anchored by two flagship products: **Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA)**, which secures outbound traffic to the internet and SaaS applications, and **Zscaler Private Access (ZPA)**, which provides identity-aware access to private applications as a modern replacement for VPNs. Around this core, Zscaler has layered data protection, cloud sandboxing, a Zero Trust Firewall, browser isolation, digital experience monitoring (ZDX), and a growing suite of AI-era security products including Zero Trust for AI Agents, AI Protect, and the ZAgent framework for agentic administration.

Revenue is almost entirely subscription-based and recurring, sold on multi-year contracts and increasingly through flexible consumption arrangements (Z-Flex) that expand deal size. The company serves a large share of the Fortune 500 and reports strong dollar-based net retention driven by upsell of additional modules and users.

The competitive moat rests on scale, switching costs, and a data advantage. Zscaler's cloud sees an enormous volume of traffic, which feeds its ThreatLabz threat intelligence and improves detection. Once an enterprise routes its traffic through the Zero Trust Exchange and configures policy, migrating away is disruptive and costly. Principal competitors include Palo Alto Networks (Prisma Access), Cisco, Netskope, Cloudflare, and Microsoft's Entra and security stack, as well as endpoint-led platforms like CrowdStrike expanding into adjacent categories.

Management is led by founder, Chairman, and CEO Jay Chaudhry, a serial security entrepreneur who retains significant ownership and vision-setting influence, alongside CFO Kevin Rubin. The company has recently navigated go-to-market leadership changes, a factor investors are watching closely.

## Financial Analysis

Zscaler's fiscal year ends July 31. For **FY2025** (ended July 31, 2025), revenue was approximately \$2.67B, up about 23% year-over-year, with a GAAP net loss of roughly \$41.5M driven largely by heavy stock-based compensation. Gross margins remain best-in-class at approximately 80%, and the company generates substantial free cash flow — roughly \$1.0B in FY2025, a free cash flow margin above 30%.

Through the first three quarters of **FY2026**, momentum has stayed strong:

- **Q3 FY2026 revenue:** \$850M, up about 25% year-over-year and 4% sequentially, above the high end of guidance.
- **ARR:** grew about 25% (21% excluding the Red Canary acquisition), surpassing \$3.2B and tracking toward the raised full-year target.
- **Non-GAAP operating margin:** reached an all-time high of about 23%.
- **Free cash flow margin:** roughly 29% year-to-date.

Management raised full-year FY2026 guidance after Q3 to ARR of \$3.740–\$3.749B, revenue of \$3.3295–\$3.3325B (about 24.6–24.7% growth), non-GAAP operating income of \$755–\$757M, and non-GAAP EPS of \$4.10–\$4.11.

On a trailing-twelve-month basis, revenue is about \$3.17B (+24.6%), with a GAAP net loss near \$77M and diluted EPS around -\$0.49; the GAAP loss reflects stock-based compensation rather than cash burn. The balance sheet is strong, with roughly \$3.0B in cash and investments and manageable convertible debt. Shares outstanding are approximately 162M, giving a market capitalization near \$25.0B at the current price.

## Valuation

At \$154.46, Zscaler trades at roughly 35x forward (non-GAAP) earnings and around 7–8x forward revenue — a meaningful compression from the double-digit revenue multiples the stock commanded at its highs. The 52-week range of \$114.63 to \$336.99 illustrates just how far sentiment has swung; the stock now sits closer to its low than its high despite continued mid-20% growth and record profitability.

The analyst community remains constructive: the consensus rating is Buy across roughly 46 analysts, with an average 12-month price target near \$192.55, implying about 25% upside from the current price. Recent individual targets have ranged from KeyBanc's \$176–\$185 (Overweight) to Guggenheim's \$214 (upgraded to Buy on the selloff).

Scenario analysis:

- **Bull case:** \$220–\$260. ARR reaccelerates as Zero Trust Everywhere, data security, and AI security cross-sell into the installed base; Zero Trust for AI Agents, AI Protect, and ZAgent establish Zscaler as the security layer for enterprise agentic AI; margins keep expanding on Rule-of-50-plus performance; and FY2027 guidance clears the lowered bar, re-rating the multiple toward premium security peers.
- **Base case:** \$180–\$200. Zscaler sustains mid-20% ARR and revenue growth with steady margin expansion, integrates Red Canary and Symmetry Systems, and delivers FY2027 guidance roughly in line with expectations. The stock trades toward the consensus target near \$192 at a reasonable forward multiple.
- **Bear case:** \$110–\$130. FY2027 guidance disappoints, growth decelerates below 20% as large-enterprise deals lengthen, competition compresses pricing, and elevated stock-based compensation keeps GAAP results negative, pressuring the multiple back toward the 52-week low.

## Growth Catalysts

Zscaler has several durable growth levers:

1. **Zero trust replacement cycle.** Enterprises continue to retire legacy VPNs, firewalls, and MPLS networks in favor of cloud-delivered SSE and SASE. Zscaler is a category leader positioned to capture this multi-year architectural shift.
2. **Platform cross-sell.** Beyond ZIA and ZPA, the company upsells data protection, ZDX digital experience monitoring, and workload segmentation, driving strong net retention and larger deal sizes via Z-Flex consumption pricing.
3. **Securing AI.** Zscaler is extending zero trust to AI: Zero Trust for AI Agents, AI Protect, and the ZAgent framework aim to govern how AI agents access data and interact with systems — a large, emerging attack surface that plays directly to Zscaler's inline-inspection strengths.
4. **Data security expansion.** Acquisitions such as Symmetry Systems (data security posture management) and Red Canary (managed detection and response / SecOps) broaden the platform beyond access into data and threat operations.
5. **International and public-sector reach.** Sovereign-cloud partnerships (for example with Schwarz Digits in Europe) and FedRAMP-authorized offerings extend the addressable market.
6. **Margin leverage.** With record non-GAAP operating margins and ~30% free cash flow margins, incremental ARR increasingly drops to the bottom line.

## Risk Factors

- **FY2027 guidance risk.** Management struck a cautious tone on the FY2027 outlook amid leadership changes and new product launches; the September 2026 print is a binary near-term catalyst.
- **Competition.** Palo Alto Networks, Cisco, Netskope, Cloudflare, Microsoft, and CrowdStrike are all investing heavily in overlapping SASE, zero trust, and data security capabilities, pressuring pricing and win rates.
- **Growth deceleration.** The law of large numbers makes sustaining mid-20% growth harder; any dip below 20% could compress the multiple sharply.
- **Stock-based compensation and GAAP losses.** Heavy SBC keeps GAAP results negative and dilutes shareholders even as cash generation is strong.
- **Execution and leadership transition.** Go-to-market leadership changes introduce sales-productivity risk.
- **Valuation.** Even after the selloff, a ~35x forward multiple leaves limited room for missteps.
- **Macro and IT budgets.** Cybersecurity spending is resilient but not immune to enterprise budget tightening.

## Recommendation

Rating: **BUY**. Zscaler pairs a durable, category-leading zero trust franchise with mid-20% ARR growth, record non-GAAP operating margins, and roughly 30% free cash flow margins. The stock has de-rated meaningfully from its highs on FY2027 caution and leadership-transition worries, and now trades near \$154 versus an analyst consensus target around \$192.55 (about 25% upside). That combination of secular growth, improving profitability, and a compressed multiple skews the risk/reward positively.

Position sizing: a medium position (2–4% of a growth-oriented portfolio) is reasonable for investors comfortable with a binary September catalyst. Given the elevated event risk around FY2027 guidance, consider scaling in rather than taking a full position at once.

Entry strategy: accumulating in the \$140–\$155 zone offers a favorable setup, with the 200-day moving average near \$153 acting as a pivot; a reclaim and hold above the \$150s would confirm improving momentum. The \$114–\$120 area (near the 52-week low) is the key long-term support and would be an aggressive add zone. Momentum indicators such as RSI have swung between oversold conditions during the selloff and neutral readings on the recent bounce.

Stop-loss: a decisive close below the \$114 52-week low would invalidate the bullish thesis and signal a deeper deceleration, warranting a reduction in exposure.

## Sentiment Analysis

Sentiment toward Zscaler is cautiously improving off depressed levels. The stock was punished after Q3 FY2026 earnings as investors focused on slower-than-expected growth and cautious FY2027 commentary, then found support as several analysts argued the selloff was overdone — Guggenheim upgraded to Buy with a \$214 target, and KeyBanc reiterated Overweight. The consensus rating across roughly 46 analysts is Buy, with an average target near \$192.55.

News flow has centered on Zscaler's AI-security positioning ("the cybersecurity platform for the AI era"), an expanding partner ecosystem (Gigamon, Saviynt, Radiant Logic, Schwarz Digits), and high-profile branding such as the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One partnership. Social and options sentiment reflects elevated implied volatility into the September 1, 2026 earnings date, consistent with a large expected move.

Overall sentiment score: **Neutral to moderately positive** — strong fundamentals and a compelling valuation reset offset by a binary near-term guidance catalyst and lingering execution questions.

## Readability Pass

Zscaler runs a giant cloud that sits between a company's employees, devices, AI agents, and the apps they use — inspecting all that traffic to block threats and stop data leaks. This "zero trust" approach replaces old-school firewalls and VPNs, and big enterprises are steadily switching over. The business is growing recurring revenue in the mid-20% range, is very profitable on a cash basis, and is now building tools to secure AI agents. The stock fell hard from its highs because management sounded cautious about next year and there were leadership changes, but analysts still see meaningful upside from today's price near \$154. We rate it BUY, with the September 1, 2026 earnings report as the big event to watch.
