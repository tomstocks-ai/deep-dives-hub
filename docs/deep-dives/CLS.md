## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Celestica is a Canadian-based technology manufacturing and supply chain solutions company that has transformed into a critical AI data center infrastructure partner. The company operates through two segments: Advanced Technology Solutions (ATS) and Connectivity & Cloud Solutions (CCS), with CCS now the dominant growth engine driven by AI networking switches, server rack integration, and high-power compute platforms.

**Revenue Segment Breakdown:**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Connectivity & Cloud Solutions (CCS)** | ~75% | AI/ML servers, networking switches (400G/800G), storage solutions |
| **Advanced Technology Solutions (ATS)** | ~25% | Industrial, aerospace & defense, health tech manufacturing |

### Industry Position and Competitive Moat

**Competitive Advantages:**

- Deep engineering partnerships with hyperscalers for custom AI server and networking switch design/manufacturing
- Rapid 400G-to-800G networking switch transition expertise
- Vertically integrated design, engineering, and manufacturing capabilities
- Geographic diversification across North America, Asia, and Europe

**Competitive Moat Assessment:** MODERATE — engineering expertise creates switching costs, but EMS/ODM industry is competitive
