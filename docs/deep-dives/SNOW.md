# SNOW — Snowflake Inc.

Snowflake is the leading cloud-agnostic data platform, and the deeply-oversold recovery thesis we flagged in April has fully played out. Shares have roughly doubled from ~\$119 (RSI ~16) to ~\$273 on sustained 30%+ revenue growth, positive free cash flow, and accelerating AI Data Cloud adoption. With a 51-analyst Strong Buy consensus and a durable profitable-cash-flow profile, we upgrade the stock from SPEC. BUY to **BUY** and drop the speculative tag — while acknowledging that a full ~129x forward-earnings valuation now leaves only modest near-term upside.

**Rating:** BUY  ·  **Price:** \$272.92 (2026-07-28)  ·  **Market Cap:** ~\$94.6B  ·  **52-Week Range:** \$118.30 – \$284.99

---

## Company Overview

Snowflake operates a **consumption-based data cloud platform** that lets organizations store, process, share, and analyze massive datasets across all three major public clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP). Its architecture separates storage from compute, allowing customers to scale each independently and pay only for what they use.

**Revenue model**

- **Product revenue (~95% of total):** consumption fees for storage, compute credits, and data transfer — revenue scales with usage rather than fixed seats.
- **Professional services (~5%):** implementation, training, and consulting.

Because revenue tracks usage, Snowflake's incentives are aligned with customer value — but the model also introduces quarter-to-quarter volatility when enterprises adjust data-processing budgets.

**Competitive moat**

- **Multi-cloud abstraction** — the most genuinely cloud-agnostic platform among the majors.
- **Data-sharing network effects** — a large customer base can share governed data seamlessly, deepening platform stickiness.
- **Zero-copy cloning and separation of storage and compute** — architectural advantages for cost efficiency.
- **AI Data Cloud** — Cortex AI and Snowflake Intelligence extend the platform from "analyze your data" to "build AI applications on your data," supported by a broad partner ecosystem (including the Modern Marketing Data Stack).

**Management**

CEO **Sridhar Ramaswamy** (former Google Ads SVP, ex-Neeva) has re-centered the company on AI. Notably, his compensation package is worth up to ~\$448M and is tied to the stock roughly doubling in market value — a strong alignment signal that also flags future dilution and is worth watching from a governance standpoint. The fiscal year ends January 31.

## Financial Analysis

**Income statement**

- **FY2026 (ended Jan 2026) total revenue:** \$4.68B, up **29.2% YoY** from \$3.63B.
- **FY2026 GAAP net loss:** -\$1.33B, driven largely by heavy stock-based compensation.
- **TTM revenue:** ~\$5.03B, up **31.1%**.
- **TTM GAAP net income:** -\$1.20B; **TTM diluted EPS:** -\$3.51.
- **Profitability:** non-GAAP operating margin is positive and the company is solidly **free-cash-flow positive** at **~\$1.2B+ FCF (~25% FCF margin)**.

**Key operating metrics**

- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** ~125% — customers keep spending more over time.
- **Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO):** ~\$9.8B — a large contracted backlog underpinning forward growth.
- **Cash & investments:** \$4.8B+, providing ample runway and optionality.

**Quality read-through**

The combination of 30%+ growth and a ~25% FCF margin clears the Rule of 40 comfortably. The central financial blemish remains GAAP losses fueled by stock-based compensation — real cash generation is strong, but shareholders bear ongoing dilution. The story has shifted from "oversold turnaround" to "profitable-cash-flow quality compounder."

## Growth Catalysts

- **AI Data Cloud is the engine.** Cortex AI and Snowflake Intelligence adoption is the core growth driver, extending the platform into generative-AI and agentic workloads on governed enterprise data.
- **Broad partner ecosystem.** Integrations across the data and marketing stack (including the Modern Marketing Data Stack) widen the funnel; **Citi named SNOW a top-3 software pick for 2026.**
- **Databricks read-through.** Databricks raised new funding at a **~\$188B private valuation** and expanded a **~\$100M AI deal with OpenAI.** This cuts two ways — it intensifies AI-workload competition, but Jefferies argues the private mark makes public Snowflake look relatively undervalued.
- **Large contracted backlog.** ~\$9.8B RPO and ~125% NRR provide visibility into forward consumption growth.
- **Margin expansion.** Continued non-GAAP operating-margin and FCF-margin gains can drive earnings even without multiple expansion.

---

## Risk Factors

- **Full valuation.** At ~129x forward earnings with the average target only ~9% above the price, the margin for error is thin and multiple compression is the primary downside risk.
- **Competition.** Databricks (now ~\$188B privately, with an expanded OpenAI relationship) and **Microsoft Fabric** — bundled with the Microsoft estate — compete directly for AI-data workloads; cloud-native warehouses (BigQuery, Redshift) compete on price.
- **Consumption-model volatility.** Usage-based revenue can swing quarter to quarter with enterprise budget cycles, amplifying reactions to any guidance wobble.
- **Heavy stock-based compensation.** SBC keeps GAAP results in the red and dilutes shareholders even as cash generation improves.
- **Governance / dilution.** The CEO's pay package worth up to ~\$448M, tied to the stock doubling, is strong alignment but also a dilution and governance consideration.

## Recommendation

**Rating: BUY.** The speculative-bounce thesis has converted into a quality-compounder thesis. Snowflake now pairs 30%+ growth with positive free cash flow, ~125% NRR, a ~\$9.8B backlog, and clear AI Data Cloud momentum, backed by a 51-analyst Strong Buy consensus (avg target ~\$297.60). The upgrade from SPEC. BUY drops the speculative tag.

**Position sizing and entry.** Because the stock has already doubled and trades at a full multiple, this is best accumulated on pullbacks rather than chased. Near-term upside to consensus is modest (~9%), so size the position for a multi-quarter hold and add on weakness toward the low-\$200s. Given a beta of ~1.35, expect volatility around earnings.
