# LLY — Eli Lilly and Company

Eli Lilly is the runaway leader of the incretin (GLP-1) era. Its tirzepatide franchise — Mounjaro for type 2 diabetes and Zepbound for obesity — has driven triple-digit growth, and in April 2026 Lilly extended that lead with orforglipron, the first oral small-molecule GLP-1 approved for obesity. Trailing-twelve-month revenue has reached \$72.25B (+47%) with net income of \$25.28B (+128%) and diluted EPS of \$28.15, lifting the market cap to roughly \$1.09T. The pipeline pushes the moat further out with retatrutide, a triple agonist that delivered about 56 pounds of average weight loss in Phase 3 and is slated for a Q1 2027 FDA filing, plus Kisunla (donanemab) in Alzheimer's. At ~\$1,220.66 the stock trades near the ~\$1,277 analyst consensus (Buy) target at ~34x forward earnings — a premium multiple we think category leadership justifies, though Novo Nordisk competition and litigation, US drug-pricing policy, and manufacturing execution are real risks. We rate LLY **BUY**.

## Company Overview

### Business Model and Revenue Segments

Eli Lilly and Company, founded in 1876 and headquartered in Indianapolis, discovers, develops, manufactures, and markets human pharmaceuticals worldwide across the US, Europe, China, Japan, and other international markets. The business is anchored today by **cardiometabolic health** — and specifically by the incretin (GLP-1) franchise built on tirzepatide, sold as **Mounjaro** for type 2 diabetes and **Zepbound** for obesity. These two brands have grown at triple-digit rates and are the primary engine behind Lilly's revenue and earnings surge.

**Approximate revenue drivers:**

| Franchise | Role | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| **Incretins (tirzepatide)** | Dominant growth driver | Mounjaro (T2D) and Zepbound (obesity); the core of the GLP-1 franchise |
| **Orforglipron** | Emerging growth driver | First oral small-molecule GLP-1, approved for obesity in April 2026 |
| **Diabetes (legacy)** | Stable base | Trulicity, Jardiance, Humalog / Humulin insulins, Basaglar |
| **Oncology and immunology** | Diversifier | Verzenio, Ebglyss, Taltz and related brands |
| **Neuroscience** | Newer contributor | Kisunla (donanemab) for early Alzheimer's disease |

The strategic centerpiece is the **GLP-1 franchise**. Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist that has set the efficacy bar in both diabetes and obesity, and **orforglipron** now attacks the far larger oral-medication opportunity: a once-daily pill avoids the cold-chain, injectable-device, and manufacturing constraints of peptides, opening obesity and diabetes treatment to a much broader global population.

### Competitive Moat

- **Efficacy leadership:** tirzepatide and the next-generation pipeline have consistently produced best-in-class weight-loss and glycemic results.
- **Oral optionality:** orforglipron gives Lilly a manufacturable, globally scalable pill that most rivals cannot yet match.
- **Manufacturing scale:** an aggressive multi-year US and global capacity buildout is designed to convert unmet obesity demand into revenue faster than competitors can.
- **Pipeline depth:** retatrutide (triple agonist) and a broad oncology/immunology/neuroscience portfolio extend growth beyond the current incretins.

**Key competitor:** Novo Nordisk (Wegovy, Ozempic) is the principal rival, and the two now compete directly in both the clinic and the courtroom. Moat assessment is **HIGH** — Lilly combines the leading molecules, the deepest incretin pipeline, and the manufacturing investment to defend its lead.

### Management

Lilly is led by a long-tenured, R&D-driven executive team that has overseen the transformation from a diversified pharma into the defining company of the GLP-1 era. Management has paired heavy internal investment in manufacturing capacity with disciplined pipeline prioritization, and the company employs roughly 50,000 people worldwide. Execution on capacity expansion and pipeline delivery has been the single biggest reason the stock has re-rated to a trillion-dollar-plus valuation.
