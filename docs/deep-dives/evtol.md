## Gists

### Advanced Air Mobility

**JOBY — Joby Aviation, Inc. · <span class="rating-spec-buy">SPEC. BUY</span>**

Joby Aviation is the best-capitalized pure-play in electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) air taxis, working through the final stages of FAA type certification toward commercial passenger service. A ~\$2.5B cash and short-term investment war chest, a Toyota manufacturing joint venture (plus \$500M Toyota investment), a finalized Virgin Atlantic UK deal, the Blade acquisition, and a planned Dubai launch give it a credible path to first revenue that peers lack. FY2025 revenue was \$53.43M (largely from Blade) against a ~\$930M net loss and ~\$500M annual burn, and at \$7.27 the stock sits near its 52-week low of \$6.89 after a ~60% drawdown. The main risk is binary: revenue is gated on uncertain FAA certification timing, and ongoing dilution weighs on a ~984M-share base.

**Bull:** \$18–\$25 · **Base:** \$10–\$13 · **Bear:** \$4–\$6

**ACHR — Archer Aviation Inc. · <span class="rating-spec-buy">SPEC. BUY</span>**

Archer is one of two US front-runners racing to commercialize eVTOL air taxis with its four-passenger Midnight aircraft. It is pre-revenue and losing heavily (FY2025 net loss \$618.2M), but carries the sector's strongest balance sheet — ~\$1.8B liquidity against under \$100M debt — funding FAA certification, an initial US launch, a UAE program, LA28 Olympics status, and an Anduril defense partnership. At \$4.82 versus a ~\$10.5 analyst average target, upside is large if certification lands; the key risks are certification slippage and dilution.

**Bull:** \$12–\$16 · **Base:** \$7–\$9 · **Bear:** \$2–\$3
