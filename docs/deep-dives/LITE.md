# LITE — Lumentum Holdings Inc.

Lumentum has finished the transition from cyclical telecom-component supplier to core AI infrastructure optics vendor. Fiscal 2026 revenue rose 83% to \$3.01B, non-GAAP operating margin went from 9.7% to 29.8%, and the guided December-quarter run-rate of ~\$1.25B implies roughly 130% year-over-year growth. NVIDIA's \$2B preferred-stock investment in March 2026, paired with a multi-billion purchase commitment and capacity rights, hard-coded Lumentum into the AI optics supply chain. The stock has priced a great deal of that in: at \$921.32 (2026-09-28) it is up 467% over twelve months and trades on roughly 43x forward non-GAAP earnings. Rated **SPEC. BUY** — the operating story is real and accelerating, but the entry point carries drawdown risk that the fundamentals alone do not cushion.

## Company Overview

Lumentum (NASDAQ: LITE), headquartered in San Jose, designs and manufactures lasers, optical modules and photonic subsystems. Since being spun out of JDSU in 2015 it has been a two-segment reporter:

- **Components — \$2.01B in FY2026, 66.5% of revenue, +79.7% year over year.** Indium phosphide and gallium arsenide lasers, ultra-high-power (UHP) pump lasers for co-packaged optics, externally modulated lasers, ROADM and wavelength-selective switch components, and the industrial and sensing laser lines. The Cloud Light acquisition sits here and supplies cloud transceiver modules.
- **Systems — \$1.01B in FY2026, 33.5% of revenue, +90.7% year over year.** Optical circuit switches (OCS), line systems and transport subsystems sold into hyperscale data centers and carriers.

The moat is narrow but genuine: vertically integrated indium phosphide laser fabrication is hard to replicate, qualification cycles inside hyperscaler and NVIDIA reference designs run years, and UHP laser output for co-packaged optics is currently supply-constrained industry-wide. That constraint is exactly what NVIDIA paid to secure. On 2026-03-02 NVIDIA bought 2,876,415 shares of Series A Convertible Preferred at \$695.31 per share for \$2B, alongside a non-exclusive multi-year strategic agreement carrying a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment, future capacity rights, and funding earmarked for R&D and a new U.S. fab. NVIDIA made an identical \$2B investment in rival Coherent the same day — Lumentum is a preferred supplier, not a sole one.

President and CEO Michael Hurlston, who joined from Synaptics in 2024, has driven the mix shift toward cloud and AI and has been unusually direct about the earnings reset: at Deutsche Bank's technology conference in late August 2026 he told investors the company now sees roughly \$40 of 2028 earnings power, explicitly above sell-side models.

**Income statement.** Q4 revenue of \$1.01B was up 109.3% year over year and 24.5% sequentially, beating the ~\$988M Street estimate, with non-GAAP EPS of \$3.23 against a ~\$2.97 consensus. That was the eighth consecutive quarter of top-line growth. The operating leverage is the striking part: non-GAAP operating margin expanded 2,160bps year over year on non-GAAP operating expenses of \$138.1M, only 13.7% of revenue. Full-year non-GAAP operating income of \$897.0M compares with \$160.1M in FY2025.

**The \$7.8B charge.** Q4 GAAP net loss of \$7.16B (\$84.65 per diluted share) came from a one-time, non-cash loss on debt extinguishment of \$7,756.6M. Lumentum settled its 2026, 2028 and 2029 convertible notes in stock; because the share price sat far above the conversion prices, the conversion value in excess of principal ran to \$7.8B on roughly \$1.1B of principal. It is a real transfer of value to noteholders and a real source of dilution, but it consumed no cash and does not recur. The accumulated deficit of \$7,796.3M on the balance sheet is the residue, which is why GAAP P/E screens on LITE are meaningless right now.

**Balance sheet.** Cash and short-term investments of \$2.74B at year-end, up \$1.9B year over year on the NVIDIA placement, down \$433.9M sequentially as converts were settled. Additional paid-in capital ballooned from \$1.99B to \$12.43B; total stockholders' equity rose to \$4.64B. Long-term debt collapsed from \$2.56B to \$40.5M, but \$1,596.9M moved into current portion of long-term debt — the 2032 notes and residual converts, with capped calls providing partial dilution protection. Inventories of \$691.6M and receivables of \$520.3M both grew more slowly than the 109% revenue increase, which is the right direction. Property, plant and equipment rose from \$726.4M to \$1,159.1M as capacity was added; Q4 capex alone was \$167M.

**Cash flow.** Q4 operating cash flow was \$363.0M versus \$64.0M a year earlier. Free cash flow turned positive in FY2026 at roughly \$300M after two years of burn — third-party computations of the figure range from ~\$300M to ~\$343M depending on capex treatment, so treat it as approximate; the company does not headline a free cash flow number.

**Share count.** Common shares outstanding went from 69.8M to 88.6M in one year, plus 2.9M preferred. Q4 non-GAAP diluted shares were 101.1M. Per-share growth has therefore lagged absolute growth, and will continue to until the remaining converts are resolved.

## Valuation

At \$921.32 the market cap is ~\$83B on 89.7M shares outstanding, or closer to \$93B on the ~101M fully diluted non-GAAP share count.

- **Trailing.** 30.6x FY2026 revenue of \$3.01B; 106x FY2026 non-GAAP EPS of \$8.67. Trailing GAAP is not usable.
- **Forward.** The Q1 FY2027 guide of \$4.05–\$4.35 non-GAAP EPS annualises to roughly \$17 before any further sequential growth. Street models cluster near \$19–\$22 for FY2027 — an estimate, not company-guided — which puts the stock at roughly 43x forward earnings. That is down from a peak near 80x earlier in 2026 and ~60x in December 2025, because estimates have risen faster than the share price.
- **Street targets.** Published means range from \$1,066.87 (MarketBeat, 22–26 analysts) to ~\$1,149 (S&P Global poll of 26). Citi sits at the top at \$1,400 on the OCS opportunity, which it frames as an ~\$11B pool shared with Coherent. Recent low targets are near \$820; a \$240 target still appearing in some screens is stale.

**Bull: \$1,350–\$1,600.** Management's ~\$40 CY2028 EPS power is achieved and capitalised at 34–40x. Requires OCS to become a multi-billion line, 1.6T modules to ramp, UHP CPO lasers and ELS/NPO in-rack optics to convert engagements into volume, and operating margin to hold above 40%.

**Base: \$1,000–\$1,150.** FY2027 revenue roughly doubles toward the \$5B area on the guided run-rate, non-GAAP operating margin holds near 40%, FY2027 non-GAAP EPS lands around \$19–\$22, and the market pays ~50x. This brackets the published analyst mean targets and assumes no hyperscaler capex pause plus orderly settlement of the remaining \$1.6B of converts.

**Bear: \$450–\$600.** AI optics orders digest after the 2026 pull-forward, the multiple compresses toward 20–25x, and either an NVIDIA roadmap change or transceiver ASP erosion clips the margin story. Note this is roughly a 40–50% drawdown from spot and still leaves the stock above its \$147.81 fifty-two-week low.

## Growth Catalysts

- **Q1 FY2027 results, estimated 2026-11-03.** Guided to \$1.225–\$1.275B revenue, 39.5–40.5% non-GAAP operating margin and \$4.05–\$4.35 non-GAAP EPS. Hurlston said this reaches the company's long-term target model more than a quarter early. It is the first proof point for the reset.
- **Optical circuit switch conversion, 2H CY2026.** OCS hit a \$10M quarter three months ahead of plan in Q2 FY2026 with an order backlog described as well past \$400M, most of it slated to ship in the second half of calendar 2026. OCS is the single line item the bulls and Citi are underwriting.
- **1.6T cloud modules, FY2027.** Adoption is described as advancing; the cloud module business inherited from Cloud Light is the volume lever.
- **In-rack optics: CPO, ELS, NPO.** Rising demand for ultra-high-power co-packaged-optics lasers, an initial ELS module order, and a breadth of near-package-optics engagements are, in management's words, the first signs optics are penetrating in-rack connectivity — the TAM expansion that distinguishes this cycle from prior transceiver cycles.
- **New U.S. fab, FY2027–FY2028.** Funded by NVIDIA's \$2B. Domestic capacity also reduces tariff and export-control exposure, a live concern given U.S. restrictions on Chinese optical suppliers.
- **Capital structure clean-up.** Settling the remaining ~\$1.6B of converts fixes the share count and removes an overhang on per-share math.
- **CY2028 earnings power of ~\$40.** Management's own framing, above sell-side. Each incremental confirmation re-rates the out-year multiple.

## Risk Factors

**Valuation and positioning.** Up 467% in twelve months, 26% above the \$732.03 two-hundred-day moving average, ~43x forward earnings. The fifty-two-week range of \$147.81–\$1,085.68 tells you the realised volatility. Stocks priced on scale-up ambitions that have not shipped yet do not tolerate guidance wobbles.

**Concentration.** NVIDIA plus a short list of hyperscalers. A multi-billion purchase commitment is a strong signal, not a delivered volume, and NVIDIA funded Coherent identically — dual-sourcing is the explicit design. A roadmap change at one customer resets the model.

**AI capex cyclicality.** Optical components have historically been among the first line items cut when data center spending pauses. The 109% growth rate is itself evidence of order pull-forward risk.

**Competition and ASP erosion.** Coherent, Innolight, Eoptolink and Applied Optoelectronics all want the same sockets. Transceiver pricing declines structurally; the 50.4% gross margin reflects a favourable mix that mix can move away from. Management explicitly flags broad gross margin ranges across the portfolio as a source of quarter-to-quarter volatility.

**Execution.** Capex above \$150M per quarter, a new fab, and yield ramps on OCS, UHP lasers and CPO simultaneously. Lumentum's own risk factors cite supplier and contract-manufacturer capacity, rare earth mineral availability, and tariff and export-control regimes.

**Dilution.** 69.8M to 88.6M common shares plus 2.9M preferred in one year, with \$1.6B of converts still outstanding. Absolute earnings growth has been diluted meaningfully on the way to per-share growth.

**GAAP presentation.** The \$7.8B charge and \$7.8B accumulated deficit mean automated screens, index methodologies and some risk systems will misread this company for several quarters.

**Insider supply.** Sustained selling through the rally: the President of Global Business Units sold roughly \$3.9M between 2026-08-24 and 2026-09-04 and a further ~\$1.42M across three transactions into 2026-09-25, and the General Counsel disposed of more than \$10M over a similar stretch. All under Rule 10b5-1 plans, so not a signal by itself, but it is real supply.

## Recommendation

**SPEC. BUY.** The fundamental trajectory justifies ownership; the entry price demands discipline about sizing.

**Position sizing.** Treat as a high-beta AI infrastructure satellite, not a core holding — 1–3% of a diversified portfolio. Anyone already holding AAOI, CRDO, COHR or FN should size LITE against that combined optics exposure rather than in isolation; these names correlate tightly on hyperscaler capex headlines.

**Entry strategy.** Spot sits between the \$862.84 fifty-day and well above the \$732.03 two-hundred-day moving averages, having pulled back roughly 15% from the \$1,085.68 high. Scaling in thirds is more defensible than a single fill: one tranche here, one on a retest of the fifty-day near \$860, one reserved for a flush toward the two-hundred-day. Buying immediately ahead of the estimated 2026-11-03 print is a coin-flip on a name that moved 16% on the last report.

**Stop loss.** A close below the two-hundred-day moving average (~\$730) breaks the uptrend that has been intact all year and is a reasonable technical invalidation, roughly 21% below spot. Fundamental invalidation is cleaner: any quarter where non-GAAP operating margin retreats below the mid-30s, or where OCS backlog conversion slips, is a thesis break regardless of price.

**Technical context.** RSI has spent much of 2026 in overbought territory, which in a trending name is confirmation rather than a sell signal; the more useful read is that price is 26% above the two-hundred-day, so mean reversion alone can produce a 20% drawdown without any fundamental news. Support: \$862 (fifty-day), \$730 (two-hundred-day). Resistance: \$1,085.68 (fifty-two-week high).

**Catalyst calendar.** Q1 FY2027 results estimated 2026-11-03; OCS shipments through 2H CY2026; 1.6T module and ELS/NPO ramp across FY2027; convert settlement ongoing; CY2028 \$40 EPS power as the long-dated anchor.

## Sentiment Analysis

**Analyst tone — strongly positive, and still rising.** Coverage has grown from 23 analysts in March 2026 to 26 today, split roughly 17 buys, 5 outperforms and 4 holds with no sells. The mean target has climbed from \$268 in December 2025 to \$696 in March, \$1,111 in June and \$1,066–\$1,149 now depending on the compiler. Citi raised to \$1,400 from \$1,200 on the optical circuit switch thesis; Needham reiterated Buy at \$1,040; Northland raised to \$1,070. The pattern is analysts chasing rather than capping — the mean target has risen every quarter of 2026 even as the stock doubled.

**News tone — positive.** Coverage since the August print has been about earnings power resets, OCS momentum and TAM expansion. Notably, when Lumentum and Coherent both beat in mid-August, LITE rallied and COHR slipped 4% — the market is discriminating within AI optics rather than treating it as one trade. A separate session in mid-September saw LITE jump nearly 10% while Corning barely moved, reinforcing that.

**Institutional and short positioning.** Institutional ownership is ~94.05% of shares. Short interest was 7.12M shares as of 2026-08-31, 7.97% of public float, down 2.04% from the prior report — elevated for a large-cap momentum name but shrinking, so shorts are not the marginal driver. Inverse LITE ETFs have launched, which is itself a sign the name has become a retail battleground.

**Retail and social tone — enthusiastic, crowded.** LITE is a consensus long in the AI optics cohort alongside AAOI and COHR. That is a risk factor, not a support: crowded momentum names unwind together.

**Insider activity — consistently negative.** Multiple executives selling into the rally under 10b5-1 plans, with the stock down 6.4% on 2026-08-28 and 2.2% on 2026-09-28 on insider-sale headlines. The President of Global Business Units still holds 109,127 shares after selling, so this reads as diversification rather than exit.

**Options flow.** Not verified in this research pass — no reliable public options positioning data was located, so no claim is made.

**Composite: 7.5/10 bullish.** Fundamentals and analyst revisions point one way; valuation, insider supply and crowding are the counterweights.

## Readability Pass

Lumentum makes the lasers and optical parts that move data around AI data centers as light instead of electricity. As AI clusters got bigger, copper wiring hit physical limits, and demand for Lumentum's products exploded: revenue nearly doubled to \$3.01B in the year to June 2026, and profit margins went from thin to excellent. NVIDIA thought this mattered enough to put \$2B into the company in March 2026 and commit to buying billions of dollars of product.

The huge loss on the income statement — \$6.9B for the year — looks alarming and is not. When the share price soared, holders of Lumentum's convertible bonds swapped them for stock, and accounting rules forced the company to book the difference between the bonds' face value and the stock's market value as a \$7.8B loss. No cash left the building. Adjusted for that, the company earned \$782M.

The catch is price. The stock is up 467% in a year and costs roughly 43 times next year's expected earnings. Management thinks it can earn about \$40 per share by 2028 — if that happens, today's price is reasonable. If AI data center spending pauses, or if NVIDIA leans on rival Coherent instead, the stock could realistically halve. That is why this is a small, speculative position rather than a core one.
